Privacy policy

Jarvis · last updated 17 September 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses and what it does with it.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (scope gmail.modify): message headers and content of the owner's own mailbox, used to sort and summarise mail, and to mark messages read or archive them when the owner approves. Jarvis does not delete or send email.
  • Google Calendar (scope calendar.readonly): the owner's own events, read-only, used to show the owner's schedule.

How data is used

Only to show the owner an organised view of their own mail and calendar. Data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else. Message details (such as sender, subject and part of the text) and calendar events may be sent to an AI model provider, such as Google Gemini, solely to classify or summarise them for the owner or to answer the owner's own questions. They are not used by Jarvis for any other purpose.

Storage

Credentials and processed results are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any third-party storage.

Retention and removal

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access stops all further reads immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
