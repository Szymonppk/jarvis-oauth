Jarvis

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Jarvis is a personal productivity tool used by one person: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Mail. Reads the owner's own Gmail inbox, sorts messages by what needs attention, and can mark messages as read or archive them after the owner approves. It never deletes and never sends email.
  • Calendar. Reads the owner's own Google Calendar, read-only, to show the day and week ahead. It never creates, changes or deletes events.

Where it runs

On the owner's own computer. Access to Google data is granted by the owner signing in with Google and approving it, and the resulting credentials are stored only on that computer.
